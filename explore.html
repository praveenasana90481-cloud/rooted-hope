Why Cancer Care?

From a personal cancer journey to a professional commitment.

My interest in psycho-oncology grew through my mother’s cancer journey. Experiencing diagnosis, treatment, recurrence, caregiving, uncertainty and loss closely showed me how much emotional support matters alongside medical care. That lived experience became the motivation to move deeper into psychology, cancer care and palliative support.

Today, my purpose is to contribute as part of a multidisciplinary care environment - supporting emotional wellbeing, caregiver resilience, communication, dignity and quality of life.
